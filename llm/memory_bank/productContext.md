# Product Context

Last updated: 2026-08-29

## Who it is for

The owner, on draft day and through the season — and, for the public
surface, anyone who wants the method rather than the board.

## The question the product answers

Not *"how many points will this player score?"* but *"is there another
player like this one still on the board?"* That is an ordinal, comparative
question, and it is answered by tier breaks — which is why the board is
tiers first and ranks second (ADR-0002).

## What good looks like

- A drafter can see at a glance where the cliff is at every position.
- Every number on the board traces to a source and a capture date.
- Where sources disagree, the board shows the disagreement rather than
  averaging it into false confidence.
- The page is usable on a phone at a draft table and printable on one page.

## What it will not do

Projections, auction values, league management, or republishing anyone
else's paid product.
